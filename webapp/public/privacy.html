Deskmates — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Deskmates is a personal, single-operator voice/chat assistant. It is not a public product and is not offered to third parties.

What it accesses

When Gmail and Google Calendar access is enabled, Deskmates reads email and calendar data belonging solely to the Google account that authorized it, in order to answer questions and perform actions requested by that same account's owner (e.g. summarizing recent emails, checking upcoming events, marking a message as read).

What it does not do

Deskmates does not sell, share, or transmit this data to any third party, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any model. Data is used only to serve the account owner's own requests in real time.

Storage

Deskmates does not maintain a separate persistent copy of your email or calendar contents. Conversation transcripts and logs generated in the course of normal operation are stored on the operator's own private server and are not shared externally.
